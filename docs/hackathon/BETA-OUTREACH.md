# Beta User Outreach Kit

Use these templates to get 10–20 real users before the August 17 deadline. Judges weight **business viability** heavily — even 5 paying users beats a polished demo with zero traction.

---

## Who to target

1. CS students in India (your ICP) — college discords, WhatsApp groups
2. Early-career developers learning on the job
3. Friends who alt-tab to ChatGPT constantly (honest use case)
4. Presentation-heavy roles (frame as "invisible teleprompter" for client calls)

**Do NOT position as:** interview cheating, exam help, proctoring evasion.
