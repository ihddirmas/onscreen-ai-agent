# Parakeet.ai — Positioning

**Core benefit (one sentence):** Parakeet turns whatever's on your screen or in your ear into an instant answer or dictated text, in the language you actually think in, without you leaving the app you're in.

**Positioning statement:** Parakeet helps Hinglish-speaking students and early-career workers in India get instant answers and hands-free dictation across every app by turning one hotkey into a screen-reading, audio-listening AI agent that streams answers onto a private overlay — no alt-tabbing, no translating your thoughts into English first.

**Campaign angle — "The tab-switch tax":** Every screenshot-into-ChatGPT, every alt-tab to Google mid-call, every "let me type this in English because the app doesn't get Hinglish" moment is a tax paid dozens of times a day without anyone noticing it. Parakeet removes that tax at the exact moment it happens — one hotkey, wherever you already are.

## Audience insights

1. Most dictation tools (Wispr Flow, OS voice typing) are English-first — users have to mentally translate a Hinglish thought before they can even start dictating. Parakeet defaults to Roman Hinglish output, so that translation step disappears.
2. Screenshotting an error or question into ChatGPT and alt-tabbing back is such an ingrained habit that people don't register it as friction until it's gone.
3. On a call (viva, interview, client demo), needing to look something up mid-conversation forces a choice between falling behind or visibly checking something. An assistant only the user's own eyes can see removes that tradeoff — framed honestly as call-privacy, not concealment during an evaluation.

## Competitor map

1. **Wispr Flow** — polished English-first dictation; no Hinglish, no screen Q&A, no call-audio capture, subscription-only.
2. **Windows/macOS built-in voice typing** — free but single-language, dictation-only; no screen understanding, no tool use.
3. **ChatGPT (manual screenshot workflow)** — powerful model, zero automation: screenshot, alt-tab, paste, wait, alt-tab back.
4. **Otter.ai / Zoom AI Companion** — meeting transcription/summary after the fact; no live screen Q&A, no dictation-to-cursor, no Hinglish-first STT.

Gap Parakeet owns: the only one of these unifying Hinglish-first dictation + instant screen Q&A + live call audio behind one hotkey set, in one lightweight process.

## Tone profile (locked)

Confident, direct, a little cheeky — indie-hacker energy, not enterprise SaaS. Short sentences, specific hotkeys and mechanisms over adjectives. No "supercharge," no "seamless," no "game-changing."

## Standing guardrails (see copy-review.md for full detail)

- **Windows only** for download CTAs — no macOS build artifact exists in the repo (`packaging/parakeet.spec` produces `dist/Parakeet.exe` only).
- **Hosted/Pro tier is not a live purchase** — the website states "Payments coming soon — Pro is a placeholder for now." Frame as "join the cohort," never "buy now," until checkout ships.
- **Screen-share-invisible overlay** is framed strictly as demo/client-call privacy. Never interview/exam/proctoring-evasion language, even implied.
