## Problem
Purpose: name concrete friction the audience already lives with daily

1. You hit an error, screenshot it, alt-tab to ChatGPT, paste, wait, alt-tab back — and lose the thread of what you were doing.
2. You think in Hinglish but type in stiff English, because every dictation tool assumes you think in one language.
3. You're on a call — a viva, an interview, a client demo — and need to look something up without going silent or looking away.
4. You don't want an AI assistant popping up on someone else's screen mid-share, so you close it before every call and reopen it after.

*Notes: All four are concrete, observable behaviors — no abstract "productivity loss" filler.*

## Solution
Purpose: map each pain point to the exact mechanism that removes it

1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Space. Parakeet screenshots your screen and answers immediately — zero typing required.
2. Hold Ctrl+Shift+D and speak the way you actually talk. "Kal ka weather check karo" transcribes and works as-is — Hinglish is the default, not a workaround.
3. Hold Ctrl+Shift+M during any call, video, or lecture. Parakeet hears your mic and the call audio at once and answers without you breaking focus.
4. The overlay is invisible in every screen share and recording by default on Windows — visible to you, not to whoever you're sharing with.

*Notes: 1:1 mapping to Problem section maintained for consistency check.*

## Features
Purpose: name the shipped capabilities, one benefit line each

1. **Hinglish-first dictation** (hold Ctrl+Shift+D) — speak in Hinglish, Hindi, or English; text lands at your cursor, in any app.
2. **Instant screen Q&A** (press Ctrl+Shift+Space) — one press, an immediate answer, then keep asking by typing with the screen context retained.
3. **Meeting copilot** (hold Ctrl+Shift+M) — hears your mic and system/call audio together, live, for Zoom, Meet, or a video.
4. **Keyless web search** — "search the web for X" returns cited sources via DuckDuckGo out of the box; add a free Tavily key later for higher-quality results.
5. **Screen-share-invisible overlay** (Windows) — on by default, one toggle to turn off for your own demos or recordings.

*Notes: All five map directly to shipped, verified features. No document-search or autonomous-browsing claims included.*

## How it works
Purpose: 3-step visual-friendly flow

1. Press or hold your hotkey — Space to ask about your screen, D to dictate, M during a call.
2. Parakeet captures exactly what it needs: a screenshot, your voice, or your voice plus the call audio.
3. The answer streams onto a floating, transparent overlay — or types straight into whatever text box you clicked into.

*Notes: Deliberately simple for a product screenshot/GIF sequence.*
