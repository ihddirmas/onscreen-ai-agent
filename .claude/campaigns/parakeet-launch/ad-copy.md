# Parakeet.ai — Ad Copy

## Variant 1 — Hinglish dictation (students / WhatsApp)

- **Short headline:** Speak Hinglish. It types itself.
- **Long headline:** Hold one key, speak in Hinglish, watch it land in your WhatsApp.
- **Body:** Parakeet dictates straight into any app — WhatsApp, ChatGPT, a search bar — and Hinglish is the default output, not a workaround. Hold Ctrl+Shift+D, speak the way you actually talk, release. Free to try with a free Groq key.

*Notes: 44 words. Targets: students, Windows, India geo, interest in productivity/typing tools.*

## Variant 2 — Instant screen Q&A (self-study / debugging)

- **Short headline:** Press once. Get the answer.
- **Long headline:** One hotkey screenshots your screen and just answers — no typing.
- **Body:** Stuck on a coding error, a slide, a problem set? Press Ctrl+Shift+Space — Parakeet reads your screen and answers immediately, then keeps context so you can ask follow-ups by typing. No copy-pasting into ChatGPT.

*Notes: 41 words. Framed around self-study/debugging only — no exam or test-taking language anywhere. Targets: CS students, early-career devs.*

## Variant 3 — Meeting copilot (early-career / calls)

- **Short headline:** Never go silent on a call.
- **Long headline:** Hold one key during any call and get live answers, hands-free.
- **Body:** Parakeet listens to your mic and the call audio together — Zoom, Meet, a lecture, anything you hear — and answers without you breaking focus. Hold Ctrl+Shift+M, ask, keep talking.

*Notes: 34 words. Targets: interns, early-career professionals, remote-meeting-heavy roles.*

## Variant 4 — Screen-share privacy (demos / client calls)

- **Short headline:** Your copilot, not your audience's.
- **Long headline:** The overlay that never shows up in your screen share.
- **Body:** Parakeet's overlay is invisible in Zoom, Meet, Teams, and recordings by default — visible only on your own screen. Built for demos and client calls where you don't want an extra window popping up on someone else's side.

*Notes: 38 words. Scoped strictly to demos/client calls — this is the highest-risk variant in the whole campaign for drifting into interview/exam-cheating framing. Re-check the wording against that ban every time this ad is reused or repurposed.*
