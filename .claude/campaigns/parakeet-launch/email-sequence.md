- Preview: Plus: what Parakeet can't do yet — we'd rather tell you now.

Body:

> Hold Ctrl+Shift+M during any call — Zoom, Meet, a lecture, a YouTube video — and Parakeet hears your mic and the call audio together, then answers live. Useful for a client call, a study session, or a meeting you're half-tracking.
>
> One detail worth explaining properly: the overlay is invisible in screen shares and recordings by default on Windows (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, OBS, PrintScreen). That's for your own demos and client calls, where you don't want an assistant popping up on someone else's side of the screen. You can turn it off any time you want it visible — for your own recording, for example.
>
> In the interest of not oversetting expectations: Parakeet's local-file search today is filename and text matching, not semantic search over your notes/PDFs. It reads and opens files — it never deletes or moves anything. And it doesn't click through websites or fill out forms on its own; that's a deliberate limit, not a bug.
