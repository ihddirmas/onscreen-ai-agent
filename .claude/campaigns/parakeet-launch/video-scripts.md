# Parakeet.ai — Video Script

## Demo script — 75 seconds
Purpose: show all four in-scope features in order, screen-capture-native footage

**0:00–0:03 | HOOK**
Visual: real screen recording — a code error appears, user starts to screenshot it.
On-screen text: "Screenshot. Alt-tab. Paste. Wait."
VO: "This is how most of us get an AI answer. It's slow."

**0:03–0:08 | Instant screen Q&A**
Visual: same error; user holds Ctrl+Shift+Space instead; overlay streams an answer.
On-screen text: "Ctrl+Shift+Space — instant screen Q&A"
VO: "Parakeet skips all of that. One hotkey, and it just answers."

**0:08–0:16 | Follow-up context**
Visual: user types "explain more" into the overlay's input box; new answer streams, still referencing the same screenshot.
VO: "It keeps the screen context, so you keep asking — no re-screenshotting."

**0:16–0:26 | Hinglish dictation**
Visual: user clicks into a WhatsApp chat box, holds Ctrl+Shift+D, speaks "kal ka weather check karo," releases; text appears typed exactly as spoken.
On-screen text: "Ctrl+Shift+D — Hinglish dictation, by default"
VO: "Hold, speak in Hinglish — however you actually talk — release, and it's typed at your cursor. No translating into English first."

**0:26–0:36 | Meeting copilot**
Visual: user on a video call, holds Ctrl+Shift+M, asks a question aloud; overlay answers referencing both what they said and what was said on the call.
On-screen text: "Ctrl+Shift+M — hears your mic + the call"
VO: "On a call, hold this one — it hears you and the call audio together, and answers without you looking away."

**0:36–0:44 | Screen-share-invisible overlay**
Visual: split screen — the user's own monitor showing the overlay, next to a screen-share preview window where the overlay is absent.
On-screen text: "Invisible in screen shares & recordings (Windows, on by default)"
VO: "And on your own screen share, it stays yours — it doesn't show up on the other end."

**0:44–0:52 | Keyless web search + free setup**
Visual: web_search returning a cited answer with no key configured; quick cut to the Settings screen showing the free Groq key field.
On-screen text: "Keyless web search. One free key to start."
VO: "Web search needs no API key. Everything you just saw runs free with one free key."

**0:52–1:00 | CTA**
Visual: Parakeet tray icon, download button.
On-screen text: "Download free — Windows"
VO (final 5 sec, CTA): "Download Parakeet free today, and try your first hotkey."

*Notes: Visual direction — real screen-capture footage only, no stock B-roll, light quick-cut pacing, no dramatic music swells; keep 0:36–0:44 framed strictly as "your own screen share," matching the ad copy and email framing exactly.*
